Cookie policy.

Last updated: 13 October 2026. Questions: hello@astramarketing.gr.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores on your device so it can remember something between pages or visits. Similar technologies (local storage, pixels) are covered by this policy too.

1. On this website

No analytics, no advertising cookies. The pages of astramarketing.gr set no tracking cookies of their own. We do not use Google Analytics or similar tools. We only keep a small technical value in your browser's session storage to remember that the intro animation has already played; it contains no personal data and disappears when you close the tab.

2. Booking page

The "Book a call" page loads the Calendly scheduling widget (Calendly LLC). Calendly may set its own cookies to run the booking form and to remember your choices. They are set only when you open that page. Calendly's policy: calendly.com/privacy.

3. Advertising landing pages

Our advertising landing pages (served under a separate path, not the main site) use the Meta Pixel (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd) to measure how our ads perform and to show relevant ads on Facebook and Instagram. These cookies are set only on those pages. You can limit them through your browser's cookie settings or your Meta ad preferences at facebook.com/adpreferences.

4. Client portal and app

The portal and the internal app use strictly necessary storage to keep you signed in (an authentication token) and to remember your settings. These are required for the service to work and are deleted when you sign out.

5. Your choices

You can block or delete cookies from your browser settings at any time. Blocking Calendly's cookies may prevent the booking widget from working; in that case write to hello@astramarketing.gr and we will arrange the call by email.

6. Changes

We update this page when our tools change; the date at the top shows the current version. Related pages: Privacy policy and Terms of use. Greek version: Πολιτική cookies.
