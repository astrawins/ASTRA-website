What we do

We bring patients to aesthetic clinics and measure it in appointments that happened. Three services, used together or separately:

Meta & Google ads for clinics. Appointment campaigns built around one treatment at a time, inside the health advertising rules, with every lead followed up by our team.

Social media video for doctors. We script, film in your practice, edit and publish short videos that make you the doctor patients in your city already know.

Lead follow-up and booking. A sales process for your clinic: first call within minutes, a fixed follow-up sequence, booking into your calendar, reminders before the visit.

Frequently asked questions

What does astra do?

astra is a marketing agency in Athens that works only with aesthetic clinics, medical aesthetics practices and doctors. We run Meta and Google appointment campaigns, produce social media video for doctors and follow up every lead until it becomes a booked appointment.

How are you paid?

On results. The ad budget is paid by the clinic directly to the platforms. Our fee is an agreed amount per appointment that actually takes place. No appointments, no fee.

Which clinics do you work with?

Aesthetic clinics and institutes, dermatology, plastic surgery, laser and body-contouring centres, dental aesthetics, medical spas and individual doctors. Most of our clients are in Athens and Attica; we work across Greece and Cyprus.

Do I need to handle the leads myself?

No. Our team calls and texts every lead, answers questions, books the appointment and sends reminders. Your reception receives confirmed appointments.

Is advertising a clinic allowed in Greece?

Informative medical advertising is allowed within the limits set by the medical associations and the law. Every ad is written to those limits and approved by the doctor before it runs.

How quickly do we see results?

The first leads usually arrive within days. A reliable cost per appointment takes a full month of data. We agree the result we are aiming for before the first euro is spent.

How do I start?

Book a free call. We look at your clinic, your treatments and your area, and tell you honestly whether we can bring you patients and how.
