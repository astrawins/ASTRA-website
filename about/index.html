A practice, not an agency.

astra exists because aesthetic clinics keep getting marketing built for restaurants, gyms and e-shops. We chose one industry and decided to know it completely.

Why one niche

Marketing an aesthetic clinic is a specialist's job. The ad platforms restrict health advertisers. Before and after photos, the industry's most persuasive material, are banned in ads and regulated in organic content. Patients research for weeks before they book, and they judge a clinic's medicine by its typography. A generalist agency learns all of this at your expense.

Working with one industry means the patterns repeat and the playbook compounds. The seasonality of injectables, the search behavior before a rhinoplasty consult, the review reply that reassures the next reader: we have seen the shape of it before, and each clinic makes the protocol sharper.

What we hold ourselves to

  • Every euro reports back to a number you can read: the cost of a booked consultation
  • Written plans before spend. You always know what runs, where, and why
  • Claims stay inside medical advertising rules. No exceptions for performance
  • No invented numbers, ever. We are a new practice and we say so; the work carries the argument
  • One point of contact who knows your clinic, not a ticket queue
