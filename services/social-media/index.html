What this fixes

Patients choose doctors they feel they already know. A profile with stock photos and a treatment menu does not do that. Short videos of the doctor answering the questions patients ask every day do, and the platforms push them far beyond the clinic's followers.

The problem is time. A doctor cannot script, film, subtitle and post twelve videos a month and run a practice. So we do it: one filming session in your clinic per month, a full month of vertical videos cut from it, posted on a schedule, with comments and messages monitored.

The results are public. Our videos for gynecologists, ENT surgeons, psychologists and aesthetic doctors have reached tens of thousands of views each on the doctors' own profiles, organically. See the case studies on our video landing page.

How it works

One hour a month from you. Everything else from us.

Topics and scripts

We research what patients in your specialty search and ask, prepare 8 to 12 question-style topics and send them to you before filming. You approve or change them.

One filming session

We come to your practice with camera, lights and a teleprompter if you want one. In 60 to 90 minutes we record material for the whole month, with as many takes as you need.

Editing, subtitles, hooks

Each answer becomes a vertical video with a title that stops the scroll, subtitles, clean cuts and your branding. Nothing unprofessional goes out: you approve every video.

Publishing and amplification

We post on Instagram, TikTok and Facebook on a schedule, monitor comments and messages, and forward anything that needs a medical answer or is a booking request. If you want patients from a specific area, we promote the best videos with a budget we agree together.

What's included

  • Monthly topic research and scripts for your specialty
  • On-site filming in your practice, once a month
  • 8 to 12 vertical videos per month, edited, subtitled and branded
  • Publishing on Instagram, TikTok and Facebook with a content calendar
  • Comment and message monitoring, booking requests forwarded to reception

Who it is for

Doctors and clinics who want to be known in their city: gynecologists, dermatologists, plastic surgeons, ENT, dentists, psychologists and aesthetic clinics. Most of our video clients are in Athens; we also film in other Greek cities on a planned schedule.

You do not need to be comfortable on camera. None of our doctors were at first. We give you the questions in advance and keep only the best minutes.

Frequently asked questions

How many videos per month?

Usually 8 to 12 vertical videos from one filming session. We agree the pace that fits your practice on the first call.

What do I say in the videos?

What your patients ask every day. We prepare the topics based on your specialty and on what people search, and you approve them before filming.

Is this compatible with medical ethics rules?

Yes. The content is informative: no promises of results, no prices, no comparison with colleagues. The doctor approves every video before it is published.

Do I need to pay for ads too?

Not necessarily. The views in our case studies are mostly organic. If you want patients from a specific area, we promote the best videos with a budget we agree together.

Who handles the comments and messages?

We monitor comments and messages and forward anything that needs a medical answer or is a request for an appointment.

Can you also run the ads?

Yes. Many clinics combine video with our Meta and Google appointment campaigns, and the best-performing videos become the ads.
