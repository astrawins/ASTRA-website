Be the clinic patients find first.

Before anyone trusts you with their face, they search. We tune your site, your Google profile and your reviews to the searches patients run in your city.

What this fixes

A patient deciding on a treatment runs a handful of predictable searches: the treatment name, the treatment plus your city, and then the clinics themselves. If your site has one thin services page, an unclaimed or half-filled Google Business Profile, and a review count frozen since last year, that patient books elsewhere without ever knowing you existed.

Local SEO is the highest-leverage channel a clinic has, because the intent is already there. We make sure the technical foundation is sound, every treatment has a page worth ranking, and your Google profile works like a second homepage.

The protocol

Foundation first, then content, then compounding reviews.

Audit the foundation

Technical crawl of your site, current rankings, profile health, and where competitors outrank you.

Fix and claim

Technical fixes, correct categories and hours on your Google Business Profile, consistent listings across directories.

Build treatment pages

One well-made page per treatment, answering what patients actually ask before they book.

Grow reviews and read the numbers

A simple review flow your front desk can run, and a monthly readout: rankings, calls, and direction requests.
