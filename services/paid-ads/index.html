What this fixes

Most clinics have already tried ads: a boosted post, a generalist agency, a burst of clicks that never became patients. Aesthetic medicine is a restricted category and the platforms treat it that way. Meta limits how health advertisers target and measure, before-and-after imagery is banned in ads, and generic campaign structures quietly fail.

We build appointment campaigns around individual treatments: laser hair removal, injectables, skin treatments, body contouring, dental aesthetics, plastic surgery consultations. The creative pre-qualifies the patient before the click, the lead form asks only what the clinic needs, and every lead is called by our team until it becomes a booked appointment.

Then we measure the only thing a clinic can take to the bank: how many of those appointments actually happened, and what each one cost.

Frequently asked questions

What does it cost?

The ad budget is paid by the clinic directly to Meta or Google and is agreed before launch. Our own fee is performance-based: an amount per appointment that actually takes place, defined in writing before we start. No appointments, no fee.

How fast do we see appointments?

The first leads usually arrive within the first days of a campaign. A reliable picture of cost per appointment takes a full month, because the platforms need enough conversions to stabilise.

Do you use before-and-after photos?

Not in ads, because the platforms ban them for health advertisers and Greek medical advertising rules restrict them. We use them only where it is allowed, such as the clinic's own profile, and only with the clinic's approval.

Is medical advertising allowed in Greece?

Informative advertising is allowed within the limits set by the medical associations and the law. We write every ad to those limits and the doctor approves it before it runs. See our guide on what is allowed in medical advertising in Greece.

Do I have to call the leads myself?

No. Our team contacts every lead by phone and SMS, answers questions, books the appointment and sends reminders. Your reception only receives confirmed appointments.

What do you need from the clinic?

The treatment to promote, available appointment slots, a contact at reception and access to the Meta ad account. We set up everything else.
