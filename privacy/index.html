Privacy policy.

Last updated: 25 September 2026. Questions about this policy: hello@astramarketing.gr.

Who we are

astra (astramarketing.gr) is a marketing practice working with aesthetic clinics. This policy describes what personal data this website collects and how it is used.

What we collect

When you submit the contact form, we receive the details you type: your name, your clinic's name, your email address, and your message. We use them only to reply to you and to prepare for the call you requested.

This site does not set analytics or advertising cookies. If analytics are added in the future, a consent banner will be introduced first, as required by EU law.

Where your data lives

Form submissions are delivered to our email through a form processing provider. We keep correspondence for as long as a working relationship is active or being discussed, and delete it on request.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. Email hello@astramarketing.gr and we will act on it promptly.
