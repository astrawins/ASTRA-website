# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS/JS (no framework, no build step). Chosen and approved for maximal SEO and Core Web Vitals performance; deploy target is static hosting with automatic HTTPS (Cloudflare Pages or Netlify, undecided).

## Users

Owners and managers of aesthetic clinics — medspas, cosmetic dermatology, aesthetic medicine and plastic surgery practices — evaluating a marketing partner to bring them more booked appointments. They are design-literate (their own industry sells on appearance), busy, and skeptical of generic agencies. Site language is English; the company domain is Greek (.gr), so the audience spans Greek and international clinics.

## Product Purpose

Marketing agency website for **astra** (astramarketing.gr). Its job is to persuade a clinic owner that astra understands their niche, and convert them into a booked discovery call via the contact form ("Book a call"). Success = qualified form submissions.

## Positioning

Niche exclusivity: astra markets aesthetic clinics only, not everyone. The claim a generalist agency cannot truthfully copy is category focus — knowing patient-privacy-sensitive advertising, treatment seasonality, before/after content rules, and local patient acquisition for clinics. Slogan (binding): **"Helping Clinics Reach the Stars"**.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed services (exactly three; each gets its own page for SEO):
1. Paid Ads (Meta & Google) — appointment/lead campaigns
2. Social Media Management — content and channel management for clinics
3. SEO & Local SEO — Google ranking, Google Business Profile, reviews

Primary CTA: contact form ("Book a call"). Form backend undecided — build with a static-friendly handler (e.g. Formspree placeholder) the user can swap.

Site structure: multi-page — Home, three service pages, About, Contact, plus privacy page (noindex) and 404.

Hard SEO requirements (user-specified, all binding): sitemap.xml, robots.txt, unique meta titles + descriptions, canonical tags, one H1 per page, correct heading hierarchy, alt text on all images, JSON-LD schema markup (Organization/ProfessionalService, Service, BreadcrumbList), internal linking between pages, no broken links, strong Core Web Vitals, HTTPS-ready absolute URLs on https://astramarketing.gr, OG image + full social meta, selective noindex only on utility pages.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **astra**, always lowercase in the wordmark.
- Logo: `astra logo.png` (1254×1254) — white lowercase wordmark with a star cut out of the final "a", on warm beige. Binding palette anchors sampled from the logo: warm beige `#E6CDB2` background, near-white `#FEFEFE` foreground.
- Slogan: "Helping Clinics Reach the Stars" — the star is the brand motif.
- Binding aesthetic (user-stated): minimal, futuristic, and explicitly **not** looking AI-generated — no AI-purple gradients, no generic template patterns. Warmth over cold tech.
- Voice: confident, precise, no agency buzzword soup.

## Evidence on Hand

- Only asset: the logo (`astra logo.png` in project root). 
- No testimonials, case studies, client logos, metrics, pricing, or team photos exist yet. **Future work must not fabricate any of these**; use honest structural alternatives (process, promises, niche knowledge) until real proof exists.

## Product Principles

1. Niche authority over generality — every page should prove clinic-specific knowledge.
2. The site is the portfolio — its own craft is the strongest proof an agency site can offer.
3. Honest persuasion — no invented numbers, clients, or reviews.
4. Search is a channel, not an afterthought — structure and content serve the SEO plan.
5. One job per page — every page ends at "Book a call".
