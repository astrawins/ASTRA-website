# astra — Backlink strategy

Goal: authority signals for a brand-new domain, in order of effort/return.

## Month 1 — foundations (free, do once)
- **Google Business Profile** for astra itself (when an address/phone exists) — the single strongest local signal.
- **Social profiles** linking to astramarketing.gr: Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn company page, X. Consistent name/bio/link.
- **Greek directories**: vrisko.gr, xo.gr, 11888 business listing. Consistent NAP (name, address, phone).
- **Industry directories**: Clutch, Sortlist, DesignRush (agency listings, free tiers).

## Months 2-3 — niche presence
- **Guest content**: offer one practical article to Greek beauty/health media and aesthetics portals (e.g. πρακτικός οδηγός "τι επιτρέπεται στη διαφήμιση αισθητικών ιατρείων"). One good link from a relevant site beats ten directories.
- **Partnerships**: suppliers of clinic equipment/skincare brands often list "recommended partners". Ask after the first collaborations.
- **Professional associations**: aesthetic medicine associations' vendor/partner pages, where available.

## Ongoing — content that earns links (the compounding play)
Write 2-4 answer pages per quarter targeting clinic-owner searches, e.g.:
- "Can aesthetic clinics run before/after photos in ads?" (policy explainer)
- "Marketing budget benchmarks for aesthetic clinics" (once real data exists — never invented)
- "Google Ads vs Meta Ads for clinics: where to start"
These become the pages other sites cite, and they rank for long-tail searches from the exact audience.

## Rules
- No paid link schemes, no PBNs, no comment spam — a new domain has everything to lose.
- Every link should be one a human might actually click.
- Track: Search Console (links report) + a simple spreadsheet of placements.
